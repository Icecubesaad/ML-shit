# Blinded real-hair review form

For each review_id R### score 1-5 (or N/A): (1) visible hair remaining, (2) lesion boundary preservation, (3) pigmentation preservation, (4) texture preservation, (5) artificial artefacts, (6) overall image usability. Panels left-to-right are in a fixed randomized order per sheet; the mapping file must stay with the researcher, not the reviewer. No AI opinion counts as a human rating.
