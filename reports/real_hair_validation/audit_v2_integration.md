**Research prototype only. Not clinically validated and not intended for diagnosis, triage or patient care.**

Generated by the v2 audit rerun. Every number below is read from a CSV or log listed in

## 1. What changed, and why

The original run used a hair detector with held-out image specificity 0.038 (it flagged
~88% of test images as hair-present). Phase 3 retuned that detector on 395 independent,
annotated images that had never entered training or the test set:

| quantity | old frozen detector | retuned detector |
|---|---:|---:|
| held-out image specificity | 0.038 | 0.923 |
| held-out pixel Dice | 0.302 | 0.420 |

Frozen retuned config SHA-256 `9b2226e2910955b94fbe2f6a3dbc045041631d51080541902fa1270c8f3f648d`;
TUNE/HELD_OUT split SHA-256 `3fe3f2c6bf07ba0c827a70ee2c6ff0d02d104822207a8ce481ecec1f26ace7df`.
No test image, label, mask or metric entered the tuning grid.

The retuned detector then drove a fresh v2 re-screen (**622 hair-present / 9,393 hair-absent**
across the 10,015-image cohort), the v2 B and A* processed images, the Cv2 re-sampling, and
the DullRazor step in H1 image quality. A/123 and A/2026 checkpoints were absent from the
volume and were retrained with the unchanged A recipe so all four arms are matched at three
seeds; no result was invented.

## 2. H1 — hair-removal image quality (held-out synthetic pairs)

400 paired images from the *frozen test split* (200 ids × two fixed renderers: `standard`,
`hard`). Two geometry policies are reported separately and never averaged. Values are means;
the test is a 10,000-replicate lesion-group paired bootstrap, two-sided, with p floored at
2/(10,000+1) = 0.00019998.

| policy | arm | PSNR (dB) | SSIM | vs hairy PSNR (95% CI) | Holm p |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 128 px | hairy input | 29.25 | 0.827 | — | — |
| 128 px | B DullRazor | 37.81 | 0.934 | +8.57 (+7.68 to +9.45) | 0.0006 |
| 128 px | Cv2 diffusion | 35.18 | 0.917 | +5.94 (+5.34 to +6.52) | 0.0006 |
| native | hairy input | 25.73 | 0.908 | — | — |
| native | B DullRazor | 34.82 | 0.939 | +9.09 (+8.34 to +9.85) | 0.0006 |
| native | Cv2 diffusion | 32.96 | 0.865 | +7.23 (+6.66 to +7.79) | 0.0006 |

**Finding:** B (DullRazor) beats Cv2 on paired PSNR at both policies. Cv2-B is
−2.63 dB (95% CI −3.05 to −2.23) at 128 px and −1.86 dB (−2.15 to −1.58) at native,
both Holm p = 0.0006. At native resolution Cv2's SSIM (0.865) is *below* the hairy input's
(0.908), i.e. the diffusion output is less structurally similar to the clean reference than
the unmodified input. This is a negative result for the hypothesis that diffusion hair
removal is better; it is reported as found.

## 3. H2 — classification (matched arms, 3 seeds 42/123/2026)

Two-level lesion-group bootstrap: 10,000 replicates, lesion groups shared across arms,
seeds resampled within each arm; two-sided p floored as above. Holm is applied separately
within the `planned` and `secondary_control` families.

| subset | A | B | A* | Cv2 | comparison | difference (95% CI) | Holm p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| full (n=1502) | 0.751 | 0.732 | 0.740 | 0.721 | A−B | +0.019 (−0.016 to +0.056) | 0.550 |
| full | | | | | A−Cv2 | +0.030 (−0.009 to +0.074) | 0.406 |
| full | | | | | B−Cv2 | +0.011 (−0.027 to +0.046) | 0.550 |
| hair-present (n=109) | 0.614 | 0.567 | 0.578 | 0.490 | A−B | +0.047 (−0.098 to +0.176) | 0.459 |
| hair-present | | | | | A−Cv2 | +0.125 (−0.012 to +0.247) | 0.219 |
| hair-present | | | | | B−Cv2 | +0.078 (−0.023 to +0.175) | 0.247 |

**Finding:** no comparison reaches Holm p < 0.05. On the 109 hair-present test images the
point estimates favour the original images over both hair-removal arms, with Cv2 lowest,
but the CIs span zero. A null result, honestly reported; the study is not sized to resolve
differences of this magnitude. The `hair_present` subset is defined by the *retuned* detector
and was not used to choose any model or threshold.

Per-class precision/recall/F1/AUC/specificity and the confusion matrices for every
arm/seed/subset are in `phase6_classification_v2.csv` and `phase6_confusion_matrices_v2.csv`.

## 4. Explainability (corrected geometry, second Grad-CAM layer, off-centre frame)

Corrected XAI on all 1,502 test images with lesion masks, one shared image/mask geometry,
Grad-CAM at the last encoder block `ln_1` (primary) and at the patch grid (second layer,
sensitivity), attention rollout, chance baselines, and an off-centre pass (joint image/mask
shift up to ±30%). Lesion-overlap ("recall") means, over three seeds:

| arm | Grad-CAM ln_1 | Grad-CAM patch | rollout | centred-Gaussian baseline |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 0.160 | 0.308 | 0.435 | 0.468 |
| B | 0.121 | 0.340 | 0.441 | 0.468 |
| A* | 0.163 | 0.302 | 0.452 | 0.473 |
| Cv2 | 0.199 | 0.314 | 0.441 | 0.473 |

Off-centre rollout is 0.470 / 0.481 / 0.478 / 0.478 for A / B / A* / Cv2 against a
translated-Gaussian baseline of 0.357–0.359; the primary Grad-CAM ln_1 stays below its
baseline in both frames. Full per-seed values, all metrics and the 60 two-level-bootstrap
comparisons are in `phase6_xai_summary_v2.csv` and `phase6_xai_comparisons_v2.csv`.
Lesion masks are single-rater, FCN-initialised and dermatologist-corrected
(Tschandl et al.), **not expert-traced**.

## 7. Limitations and failure cases

- No paired natural-hairless reference exists, so real-photo hair removal is assessed only
  with proxies; blinded human ratings of `artifacts/review_kit/` have not been supplied.
- The hair-present test subset has 109 images / 104 lesion groups; its CIs are wide.
- The second Grad-CAM layer and the off-centre frame are sensitivity analyses, not primary
  endpoints; the primary Grad-CAM ln_1 localisation is below its chance baseline.
- The ~56.3k continuation generator and the first defective generator checkpoints are absent;
  **NOT VERIFIED** — not every historical number can be regenerated from this workspace.
- A*/fov and the blinded human-review kit remain outside this v2 rerun.
